The [[grafo/termos/annual deep brain|annual deep brain]] stimulation ([[grafo/termos/deep brain stimulation|DBS]]) Think Tank aims to create an opportunity for a multidisciplinary discussion in the field of [[grafo/termos/neuromodulation|neuromodulation]] to examine developments, opportunities and challenges in the field. The proceedings of the Sixth Annual Think Tank recapitulate progress in applications of neurotechnology, neurophysiology, and emerging techniques for the treatment of a range of psychiatric and [[grafo/termos/neurological conditions|neurological conditions]] including Parkinson's disease, [[grafo/termos/essential tremor|essential tremor]], [[grafo/termos/tourette syndrome|Tourette syndrome]], [[grafo/termos/epilepsy|epilepsy]], [[grafo/termos/cognitive disorders|cognitive disorders]], and [[grafo/termos/addiction|addiction]]. Each section of this overview provides insight about the understanding of neuromodulation for specific disease and discusses current challenges and future directions. This year's report addresses key issues in implementing advanced neurophysiological techniques, evolving use of novel modulation techniques to deliver DBS, ans improved neuroimaging techniques. The proceedings also offer insights into the new era of [[grafo/termos/brain network|brain network]] neuromodulation and connectomic DBS to define and target dysfunctional brain networks. The proceedings also focused on innovations in applications and understanding of [[grafo/termos/adaptive dbs|adaptive DBS]] (closed-loop systems), the use and applications of optogenetics in the field of [[grafo/termos/neurostimulation|neurostimulation]] and the need to develop databases for DBS indications. Finally, updates on neuroethical, legal, social, and policy issues relevant to DBS research are discussed.
