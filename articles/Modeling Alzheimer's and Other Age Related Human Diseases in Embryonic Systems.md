Modeling human disease in animals is an important strategy to discover potential methods of intervention. We suggest that there is much to be gained by employing a multi-model approach that takes advantage of different animal systems used in the laboratory simultaneously. We use the example of modeling Alzheimer's disease in [[grafo/termos/drosophila melanogaster|Drosophila melanogaster]], [[grafo/termos/caenorhabditis elegans|Caenorhabditis elegans]], and Danio rerio to illustrate how such an approach can be employed to investigate the pathophysiology of the disease.
