[[grafo/termos/neural circuits|Neural circuits]] of the [[grafo/termos/basal ganglia|basal ganglia]] are critical for motor planning and [[grafo/termos/action selection|action selection]] [1][2][3] .Two parallel basal ganglia pathways have been described 4 , which are proposed to exert opposing influences on [[grafo/termos/motor function|motor function]] [5][6][7] .According to this classical model, activation of the [[grafo/termos/direct pathway|direct pathway]] facilitates movement and activation of the [[grafo/termos/indirect pathway|indirect pathway]] inhibits movement.However, more recent anatomical and functional evidence has called into question the validity of this hypothesis [8][9][10] .Because this model has never been empirically tested, the specific function of these circuits in [[grafo/termos/behaving animals|behaving animals]] remains unknown.Here, we directly activated [[grafo/termos/basal ganglia circuitry|basal ganglia circuitry]] in vivo, using optogenetic control [11][12][13][14] of [[grafo/termos/direct and indirect pathways|direct-and indirect-pathway]] medium [[grafo/termos/spiny projection neurons|spiny projection neurons]] ([[grafo/termos/medium spiny neurons|MSNs]]), achieved through Cre-dependent viral expression of [[grafo/termos/channelrhodopsin-2|channelrhodopsin-2]] in the [[grafo/termos/striatum|striatum]] of D1-Cre and D2-Cre BAC transgenic mice.Bilateral excitation of indirect-pathway MSNs elicited a [[grafo/termos/parkinsonian states|parkinsonian state]], distinguished by increased freezing, [[grafo/termos/bradykinesia|bradykinesia]], and decreased locomotor initiations.In contrast, activation of directpathway MSNs reduced freezing and increased [[grafo/termos/locomotion|locomotion]].In a mouse model of Parkinson's disease, direct pathway activation completely rescued deficits in freezing, bradykinesia, and locomotor initiation.Taken together, our findings establish a critical role for basal ganglia circuitry in the bidirectional regulation of [[grafo/termos/motor behaviors|motor behavior]] and indicate that modulation of direct pathway circuitry may represent an [[grafo/termos/effective therapeutic|effective therapeutic]] strategy for [[grafo/termos/ameliorating parkinsonian motor|ameliorating parkinsonian motor]] deficits.To obtain selective optogenetic control of the direct and indirect pathways in vivo, we targeted striatal MSNs that form the origin of these pathways.We injected an [[grafo/termos/adeno-associated virus|adenoassociated virus]]
