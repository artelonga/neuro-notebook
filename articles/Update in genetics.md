Researchers can probe how the nervous system works using this technique and also other nonneuronal systems, including glial, muscle, cardiac and embrionic [[grafo/termos/stem cells|stem cells]].This technology consists of light-inducible [[grafo/termos/gene expression|gene expression]] in neurons to probe neural cir-cuits at the high speeds, with millisecond preci-sion, and to understand brain information pro-cessing.
