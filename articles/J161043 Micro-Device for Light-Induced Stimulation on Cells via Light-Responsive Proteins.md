We have developed a micro-device that enables to measure the [[grafo/termos/neural activity|neural activity]] of cultured cells evoked by photo stimuli at a single-cell level [[grafo/termos/spatial resolution|spatial resolution]]. The device is designed as an in-vitro model for a novel implantable device that is used in our future research to clarify the detailed mechanism of neural diseases such as [[grafo/termos/anxiety|anxiety]] disorders and Parkinson disease based on optogenetics. The device is fabricated by patterning microstructures of a pinhole array and a microelectrode array on a planer glass substrate. The single-cell level photo stimulation is achieved by laser light transmitted through pinholes that are 5 μm in their diameters. In order to evaluate the applicability of the pinholes, we investigated the spot size and strength of laser light through the pinholes.
