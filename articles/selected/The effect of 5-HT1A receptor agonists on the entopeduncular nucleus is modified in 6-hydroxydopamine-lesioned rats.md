BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE: [[grafo/termos/l-dopa|l-DOPA]] prolonged treatment leads to disabling [[grafo/termos/motor complications|motor complications]] as [[grafo/termos/dyskinesia|dyskinesia]] that could be decreased by drugs acting on 5-HT1A receptors. Since the internal segment of the [[grafo/termos/globus pallidus|globus pallidus]], homologous to the [[grafo/termos/entopeduncular nucleus|entopeduncular nucleus]] in rodents, seems to be involved in the etiopathology of [[grafo/termos/l-dopa-induced dyskinesia|l-DOPA-induced dyskinesia]], we investigated whether the entopeduncular nucleus is modulated by the 5-HT1A receptor partial and full agonists, buspirone, and 8-hydroxy-2-(di-n-propylamino)-tetralin (8-OH-DPAT) in control and [[grafo/termos/6-hydroxydopamine|6-hydroxydopamine]] (6-OHDA)-lesioned rats with or without long-term [[grafo/termos/l-dopa treatment|l-DOPA treatment]]. EXPERIMENTAL APPROACH: Extracellular single-unit electrocorticogram and [[grafo/termos/local field potentials|local field potential]] recordings under anaesthesia, immunostaining assays and optogenetic manipulation coupled to [[grafo/termos/electrophysiological recordings|electrophysiological recordings]] were performed. KEY RESULTS: Systemic buspirone reduced the entopeduncular nucleus [[grafo/termos/firing rate|firing rate]] in the sham animals and burst activity in the 6-OHDA-lesioned rats (with or without l-DOPA treatment), while local administration reduced entopeduncular nucleus activity in all the groups, regardless of DA integrity. Systemic 8-OH-DPAT also induced inhibitory effects only in the sham animals. Effects triggered by buspirone and 8-OH-DPAT were reversed by the 5-HT1A [[grafo/termos/receptor antagonist|receptor antagonist]], WAY-100635. Neither buspirone nor 8-OH-DPAT modified the low-frequency [[grafo/termos/oscillatory activity|oscillatory activity]] in the entopeduncular nucleus or its synchronization with the [[grafo/termos/motor cortex|motor cortex]]. Buspirone did not alter the response induced by [[grafo/termos/subthalamic nucleus|subthalamic nucleus]] opto-stimulation in the entopeduncular nucleus. CONCLUSION AND IMPLICATIONS: Systemic 5-HT1A [[grafo/termos/receptor activation|receptor activation]] elicits different effects on the [[grafo/termos/electrophysiological properties|electrophysiological properties]] of the entopeduncular nucleus depending on the integrity of the [[grafo/termos/nigrostriatal pathway|nigrostriatal pathway]] and it does not alter the relationship between subthalamic nucleus and entopeduncular nucleus [[grafo/termos/neuron activity|neuron activity]].
