Alzheimer's disease (AD), a multi-factorial [[grafo/termos/neurodegenerative disorders|neurodegenerative disorder]] has affected over 30 million individuals globally and these numbers are expected to increase in the coming decades. Current [[grafo/termos/therapeutic interventions|therapeutic interventions]] are largely ineffective as they focus on a single target. Development of an effective drug therapy requires a deep understanding of the various factors influencing the onset and progression of the disease. Aging and genetic factors exert a major influence on the development of AD. Other factors like post-viral infections, iron overload, gut dysbiosis, and vascular dysfunction also exacerbate the onset and progression of AD. Further, post-translational modifications in [[grafo/termos/tau|tau]], DRP1, CREB, and p65 proteins increase the disease severity through triggering [[grafo/termos/mitochondrial dysfunction|mitochondrial dysfunction]], [[grafo/termos/synaptic loss|synaptic loss]], and differential interaction of [[grafo/termos/amyloid beta|amyloid beta]] with different receptors leading to impaired intracellular signalling. With advancements in neuroscience tools, new inter-relations that aggravate AD are being discovered including pre-existing diseases and exposure to other pathogens. Simultaneously, new [[grafo/termos/therapeutic strategies|therapeutic strategies]] involving modulation of [[grafo/termos/gene expression|gene expression]] through targeted delivery or modulation with light, harnessing the [[grafo/termos/immune responses|immune response]] to promote clearance of amyloid deposits, introduction of [[grafo/termos/stem cells|stem cells]] and extracellular vesicles to replace the destroyed neurons, exploring new therapeutic molecules from plant, marine and biological sources delivered in the free state or through nanoparticles and use of non-pharmacological interventions like music, transcranial stimulation and yoga. Polypharmacology approaches involving combination of therapeutic agents are also under active investigation for superior therapeutic outcomes. This review elaborates on various disease-causing factors, their [[grafo/termos/underlying mechanisms|underlying mechanisms]], the inter-play between different disease-causing players, and emerging [[grafo/termos/therapeutic option|therapeutic options]] including those under [[grafo/termos/clinical trials|clinical trials]], for treatment of AD. The challenges involved in AD therapy and the way forward have also been discussed.
