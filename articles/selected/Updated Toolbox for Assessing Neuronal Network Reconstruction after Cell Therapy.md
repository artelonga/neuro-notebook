[[grafo/termos/cell therapy|Cell therapy]] has proven to be a promising treatment for a range of [[grafo/termos/neurological disorders|neurological disorders]], including Parkinson Disease, drug-resistant [[grafo/termos/epilepsy|epilepsy]], and [[grafo/termos/stroke|stroke]], by restoring function after brain damage. Nevertheless, evaluating the true effectiveness of these [[grafo/termos/therapeutic interventions|therapeutic interventions]] requires a deep understanding of the [[grafo/termos/functional integration|functional integration]] of grafted cells into existing [[grafo/termos/neural network|neural networks]]. This review explores a powerful arsenal of molecular techniques revolutionizing our ability to unveil functional integration of grafted cells within the host brain. From precise manipulation of [[grafo/termos/neuronal activity|neuronal activity]] to pinpoint the functional contribution of transplanted cells by using opto- and chemo-genetics, to real-time monitoring of neuronal dynamics shedding light on [[grafo/termos/functional connectivity|functional connectivity]] within the reconstructed circuits by using [[grafo/termos/genetically encoded|genetically encoded]] (calcium) indicators in vivo. Finally, structural reconstruction and mapping communication pathways between grafted and host neurons can be achieved by [[grafo/termos/monosynaptic tracing|monosynaptic tracing]] with [[grafo/termos/viral vectors|viral vectors]]. The cutting-edge toolbox presented here holds immense promise for elucidating the impact of cell therapy on [[grafo/termos/neural circuitry|neural circuitry]] and guiding the development of more [[grafo/termos/effective treatments|effective treatments]] for neurological disorders.
