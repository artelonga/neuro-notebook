Silent [[grafo/termos/synapse|synapses]] represent a unique class of synaptic connections that are non-functional at rest but possess the potential to become functional, serving as a critical reservoir for neural plasticity. Their activation mechanisms not only challenge traditional models of synaptic maturation but also provide novel insights into [[grafo/termos/brain function|brain function]] regulation and disease pathology. This article provides a systematic review of the regulatory [[grafo/termos/mechanisms underlying|mechanisms underlying]] silent synapse activation, encompassing pre-synaptic calcium signaling-mediated vesicle cycling and active zone (AZ) optimization, post-synaptic α-amino-3-hydroxy-5-methyl-4-isoxazolepropionic acid receptor (AMPAR) membrane insertion and post-synaptic density protein 95 (PSD-95) anchoring, Mg2+ blockade release by [[grafo/termos/n-methyl-d-aspartate receptor|N-methyl-D-aspartate receptor]] (NMDAR), as well as synergistic integration of upstream [[grafo/termos/signaling pathways|signaling pathways]]. Additionally, it explores the roles of [[grafo/termos/astrocytes|astrocytes]], epigenetic modifications, ubiquitin-proteasome systems, and autophagy-lysosomal systems in multi-level regulatory processes. Notably, abnormal regulation of silent synapses exhibits two contrasting pathological patterns in diseases: "desilencing impairment" (e.g., Alzheimer's disease, [[grafo/termos/depression|depression]]) and "abnormally excessive desilencing" (e.g., [[grafo/termos/drug addiction|drug addiction]], [[grafo/termos/chronic pain|chronic pain]]), which establishes a theoretical framework for targeted interventions. The study further evaluates the applicability and limitations of emerging technologies-including high-resolution imaging, single-cell omics, optogenetics, and AI-driven brain-inspired computing-in silent synapse research, while systematically summarizing clinical advancements, current challenges, and future directions, aiming to inform both fundamental neuroscience studies and [[grafo/termos/therapeutic interventions|therapeutic interventions]].
