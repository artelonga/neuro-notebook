BACKGROUND: The [[grafo/termos/entopeduncular nucleus|entopeduncular nucleus]] (EP) is a major output nucleus of the [[grafo/termos/basal ganglia|basal ganglia]] and plays a critical role in integrating motor, emotional, and behavioral processes. Although traditionally linked to [[grafo/termos/motor control|motor control]], accumulating [[grafo/termos/evidence indicates|evidence indicates]] that the EP is deeply involved in [[grafo/termos/psychiatric disorders|psychiatric disorders]], [[grafo/termos/sleep|sleep]] regulation, [[grafo/termos/addiction|addiction]], and mood-related behaviors. As the rodent homolog of the human internal [[grafo/termos/globus pallidus|Globus pallidus]] (GP) the EP represents a key translational structure bridging preclinical and clinical neuroscience. METHODS: This narrative review was conducted following established narrative review methodology. Relevant literature was identified through a structured search of major biomedical databases. Peer-reviewed experimental and clinical studies investigating EP anatomy, connectivity, [[grafo/termos/neurotransmitter systems|neurotransmitter systems]], and functional roles were included. The review synthesizes findings from mouse and [[grafo/termos/rat model|rat models]] employing optogenetic and [[grafo/termos/chemogenetics|chemogenetic]] manipulation, [[grafo/termos/electrophysiological recordings|electrophysiological recording]], neuroanatomical tracing, molecular approaches, and behavioral assays, alongside human neuroimaging, lesion, and [[grafo/termos/deep brain stimulation|deep brain stimulation]] studies. RESULTS: Evidence from [[grafo/termos/rodent models|rodent models]] demonstrates that the EP functions as a convergence hub integrating GABAergic, glutamatergic, dopaminergic, serotonergic, and endocannabinoid signaling. EP projections to the lateral habenula regulate aversive processing, addiction-related behaviors, and mood states. [[grafo/termos/cell-type specific|Cell-type-specific]] mouse studies identify entopeduncular circuits implicated in [[grafo/termos/anxiety|anxiety]] regulation. Dysregulation of EP circuits is implicated in [[grafo/termos/depression|depression]], anxiety, and [[grafo/termos/parkinsonian motor|Parkinsonian motor]] dysfunction. Clinical and preclinical [[grafo/termos/neuromodulation|neuromodulation]] studies further support the [[grafo/termos/therapeutic relevance|therapeutic relevance]] of targeting EP circuits. CONCLUSION: The EP is a multifunctional neural hub extending beyond motor control. Integrating evidence from rodent and human studies highlights its importance in psychiatric and [[grafo/termos/sleep disorders|sleep disorders]] and supports its potential as a translational [[grafo/termos/therapeutic target|therapeutic target]]. The entopeduncular nucleus (EP) functions extend beyond motor control to psychiatric regulation and sleep modulation.EP-lateral habenula projections are crucial for addiction and aversive behaviors.EP’s endocannabinoid system plays a vital role in sleep regulation and mood control.Deep brain stimulation of EP shows therapeutic potential for Parkinson’s disease and treatment-resistant depression.Novel therapeutic approaches targeting EP circuits are emerging for psychiatric disorders, including anxiety, [[grafo/termos/obsessive-compulsive disorder|Obsessive-compulsive disorder]] (OCD), and schizophrenia.Translation of preclinical findings to human applications remains a key challenge.
