This paper reviews the application of optogenetics in the study of Parkinson’s disease and discuss how optogenetics stimulates specific [[grafo/termos/neural circuits|neural circuits]] to regulate the symptoms and behaviors of Parkinson’s disease. Optogenetics technology has a higher [[grafo/termos/spatial resolution|spatial resolution]] than traditional [[grafo/termos/electrical stimulation|electrical stimulation]], and optogenetics can accurately control [[grafo/termos/specific neurons|specific neuron]] populations, providing a new perspective for studying PD and exploring potential treatments. The researchers found that selective optogenetic stimulation of the [[grafo/termos/dorsal striatum|dorsal striatum]] was sufficient to induce levodopalike [[grafo/termos/dyskinesia|dyskinesia]] in [[grafo/termos/6-hydroxydopamine|6-hydroxydopamine]] (6-OHDA)-induced PD [[grafo/termos/rat model|rat models]]. In addition, the researchers found that transplanted [[grafo/termos/dopamine neurons|dopamine neurons]] can correct some of the [[grafo/termos/motor deficits|motor deficits]] through their [[grafo/termos/neural activity|neural activity]], including [[grafo/termos/dopamine release|dopamine release]] and [[grafo/termos/synaptic transmission|synaptic transmission]]. Although the utility of optogenetic techniques in human patients is unclear, the [[grafo/termos/precise control|precise control]] of neural circuits in animal models provides an important way to understand the mechanisms and side effects of [[grafo/termos/deep brain stimulation|DBS]] therapy. The study also suggests that optogenetic stimulation of [[grafo/termos/glutamatergic neurons|glutamatergic neurons]] in the mesocerebellar nucleus may improve [[grafo/termos/motor function|motor function]] in PD mouse models, providing a new potential target for PD treatment. Finally, the article discusses how optogenetic technology can be transformed from a research tool into a treatment for PD, and how to repair damaged neural circuits through optogenetic technology to provide a more durable and [[grafo/termos/effective treatments|effective treatment]].
