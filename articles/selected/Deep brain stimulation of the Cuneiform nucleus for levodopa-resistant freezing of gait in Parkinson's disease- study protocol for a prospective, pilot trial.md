BACKGROUND: [[grafo/termos/freezing of gait|Freezing of gait]] (FOG) is a particularly debilitating [[grafo/termos/motor deficits|motor deficit]] seen in a subset of Parkinson's disease (PD) patients that is poorly responsive to standard [[grafo/termos/levodopa|levodopa]] therapy or [[grafo/termos/deep brain stimulation|deep brain stimulation]] (DBS) of established PD targets such as the [[grafo/termos/subthalamic nucleus|subthalamic nucleus]] and the [[grafo/termos/globus pallidus interna|globus pallidus interna]]. The proposal of a DBS target in the midbrain, known as the [[grafo/termos/pedunculopontine nucleus|pedunculopontine nucleus]] (PPN) to address FOG, was based on its observed pathology in PD and its hypothesized involvement in locomotor control as a part of the [[grafo/termos/mesencephalic locomotor region|mesencephalic locomotor region]], a functionally defined area of the midbrain that elicits [[grafo/termos/locomotion|locomotion]] in both intact animals and decerebrate animal preparations with [[grafo/termos/electrical stimulation|electrical stimulation]]. Initial reports of PPN DBS were met with much enthusiasm; however, subsequent studies produced mixed results, and recent meta-analysis results have been far less convincing than initially expected. A closer review of the extensive mesencephalic locomotor region (MLR) preclinical literature, including recent optogenetics studies, strongly suggests that the closely related [[grafo/termos/cuneiform nucleus|cuneiform nucleus]] (CnF), just dorsal to the PPN, may be a superior target to promote gait initiation. METHODS: We will conduct a prospective, open-label, single-arm pilot study to assess safety and feasibility of CnF DBS in PD patients with levodopa-refractory FOG. Four patients will receive CnF DBS and have gait assessments with and without DBS during a 6-month follow-up. DISCUSSION: This [[grafo/termos/paper presents|paper presents]] the study design and rationale for a pilot study investigating a novel DBS target for gait dysfunction, including targeting considerations. This pilot study is intended to support future larger scale [[grafo/termos/clinical trials|clinical trials]] investigating this target. TRIAL REGISTRATION: ClinicalTrials.gov identifier: NCT04218526 (registered January 6, 2020).
