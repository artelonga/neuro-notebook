BACKGROUND: Long-term use of [[grafo/termos/levodopa|levodopa]] for Parkinson's disease (PD) treatment is often hindered by development of [[grafo/termos/motor complications|motor complications]], including [[grafo/termos/levodopa-induced dyskinesia|levodopa-induced dyskinesia]] (LID). The [[grafo/termos/substantia nigra pars reticulata|substantia nigra pars reticulata]] (SNr) and [[grafo/termos/globus pallidus|globus pallidus]] internal segment (GPi) are the output nuclei of the [[grafo/termos/basal ganglia|basal ganglia]]. Dysregulation of SNr and GPi activity contributes to PD pathophysiology and LID. OBJECTIVE: The objective of this study was to determine whether direct modulation of SNr [[grafo/termos/gabaergic neurons|GABAergic neurons]] and SNr projections to the [[grafo/termos/pedunculopontine nucleus|pedunculopontine nucleus]] (PPN) regulates PD symptoms and LID in a mouse model. METHODS: We expressed Cre-recombinase activated [[grafo/termos/channelrhodopsin-2|channelrhodopsin-2]] (ChR2) or [[grafo/termos/halorhodopsin|halorhodopsin]] adeno-associated virus-2 (AAV2) vectors selectively in SNr GABAergic neurons of Vgat-IRES-Cre mice in a [[grafo/termos/6-hydroxydopamine|6-hydroxydopamine]] model of PD to investigate whether direct optogenetic modulation of SNr neurons or their projections to the PPN regulates PD symptoms and LID expression. The forepaw stepping task, mouse LID rating scale, and open-field [[grafo/termos/locomotion|locomotion]] were used to assess [[grafo/termos/akinesia|akinesia]] and LID to test the effect of SNr modulation. RESULTS: Akinesia was improved by suppressing SNr [[grafo/termos/neuron activity|neuron activity]] with halorhodopsin. LID was significantly reduced by increasing SNr [[grafo/termos/neuronal activity|neuronal activity]] with ChR2, which did not interfere with the antiakinetic effect of levodopa. [[grafo/termos/optical stimulation|Optical stimulation]] of ChR2 in SNr projections to the PPN recapitulated direct SNr stimulation. CONCLUSIONS: Modulation of SNr GABAergic neurons alters akinesia and LID expression in a manner consistent with the rate model of [[grafo/termos/basal ganglia circuitry|basal ganglia circuitry]]. Moreover, the projections from SNr to PPN likely mediate the antidyskinetic effect of increasing SNr neuronal activity, identifying a potential novel role for the PPN in LID. © 2023 The Authors.
