Neurological disease drives symptoms through pathological changes to circuit functions. Therefore, understanding [[grafo/termos/circuit mechanisms|circuit mechanisms]] that drive behavioral dysfunction is of critical importance for quantitative diagnosis and systematic treatment of neurological disease. Here, we describe key technologies that enable measurement and manipulation of [[grafo/termos/neural activity|neural activity]] and [[grafo/termos/neural circuits|neural circuits]]. Applying these approaches led to the discovery of [[grafo/termos/circuit mechanisms underlying|circuit mechanisms underlying]] pathological [[grafo/termos/motor behaviors|motor behavior]], [[grafo/termos/arousal|arousal]] regulation, and protein accumulation. Finally, we discuss how optogenetic [[grafo/termos/functional magnetic resonance|functional magnetic resonance]] imaging reveals global scale circuit mechanisms, and how circuit manipulations could lead to new treatments of neurological diseases.
