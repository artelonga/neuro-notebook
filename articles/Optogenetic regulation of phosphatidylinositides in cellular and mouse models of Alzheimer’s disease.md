Major disappointments in late‐stage [[grafo/termos/clinical trials|clinical trials]] have made it clear that additional [[grafo/termos/therapeutic target|therapeutic targets]] beyond [[grafo/termos/amyloid-β|amyloid β]]‐peptide (Aβ) and [[grafo/termos/tau|tau]] are urgently required for Alzheimer’s disease (AD). Phosphatidylinositol 4,5‐biphosphate [PI(4,5)P2] has previously been established as an important signaling lipid at the [[grafo/termos/synapse|synapse]] and in a mouse model of Alzheimer’s disease. We hypothesize that the level of PI(4,5)P2 in the plasma membrane may be an early and critical determinant of AD associated molecular and [[grafo/termos/behavioral deficits|behavioral deficits]] in cellular and mouse models of the disease.
