Introduction: Optogenetic [[grafo/termos/neuromodulation|neuromodulation]] describes a contemporary technique of brain modulation that has been increasingly studied, both in the field of [[grafo/termos/genetic engineering|genetic engineering]] and in neuroscience, for the treatment of diseases such as [[grafo/termos/epilepsy|epilepsy]], schizophrenia, parkinson and [[grafo/termos/essential tremor|essential tremor]]. Through it, we seek to alter neurons, making them sensitive to [[grafo/termos/light stimulation|light stimulation]]. For this, [[grafo/termos/viral vectors|viral vectors]] are used to insert opsin genes into neural tissue. Objective: to describe the most recent scientific findings related to the use of [[grafo/termos/deep brain stimulation|DBS]] using Optogenetics techniques. Methodology: use of databases, SCIELO, PUBMED, LILACS and American Association of Neurological Surgeons using the following descriptors: Genetic Engineering. Deep Brain Stimulation. Optogenetics. Rhodopsins. Results: The use and Deep Brain Stimulantion (DBS) or Pronfunda Cerebral Stimulation (ECP) for [[grafo/termos/therapeutic interventions|therapeutic intervention]] in patients with [[grafo/termos/movement disorders|movement disorders]] is performed through the insertion of a tungsten wire in specific areas of the [[grafo/termos/central nervous|central nervous]] system with the passage of electric current from microampers for milliseconds. However, over time, this causes plasticity, associated with gliosis and loss of DBS effectiveness. In addition, scientific evidence shows that cerebral neuromodulation by optogenetics in patients with [[grafo/termos/dystonia|dystonia]], [[grafo/termos/depression|depression]] and [[grafo/termos/obsessive-compulsive disorder|obsessive compulsive disorder]] (OCD) is also already a reality with significant and approved results. Conclusions: Optognetics can replace classic DBS for the treatment of several neurological comorbidities with safety and space-time precision, with minimal side effects, when compared with that technique.
